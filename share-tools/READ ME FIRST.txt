YourProject.exe
=============

1. Right-click the zip and choose "Extract All..." (don't run it from inside the zip).
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Start YourProject.bat".
   - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway".
3. Your browser opens YourProject.exe. Keep the black window open while you use it; close it when you're done.

Nothing gets installed, and everything works offline, music included. Needs Windows.

New things unlock on their own days, so come back and check every so often.
Your progress is saved in that browser, so keep using the same one.
